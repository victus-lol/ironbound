Before you ask.

You don't need

  • ✕ A gym membership
  • ✕ A coach or a plan from day one
  • ✕ An account of any kind
  • ✕ To know everything already

You do need

  • ✓ Curiosity about your own numbers
  • ✓ One logged workout to start
  • ✓ Ten minutes, three times a week
  • ✓ Willingness to chase the next tier
How are my stats actually computed?

Strength uses the Epley 1RM formula mapped to bodyweight ratio, endurance uses the Cooper 12-minute VO₂max estimate, and vitality uses the US Navy circumference body-fat method. Each result is interpolated across published athletic benchmarks — no invented points.

Is my data private? Where does it live?

Entirely on your device — localStorage with an IndexedDB mirror. One-click JSON/CSV export, validated import, offline queue. There is no server to leak from.

Do I need an account?

No. There are no accounts, no tracking, no paywall. Open the page and log your first set.

What if I miss a week?

Your daily fire may die, but your weekly streak survives one blank week per month via the freeze token — rest and travel are part of training, not failure.

Does it work offline or in a basement gym?

Yes. It's an installable PWA: log offline, everything syncs into place when you're back. No signal required under the barbell.
